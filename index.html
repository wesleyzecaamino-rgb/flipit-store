Everything to know about our ribbed orchid knitwear, brushed fleece sizing, cold-weather care, and winter drop delivery.

Our ribbed funnel-collar knits and brushed fleece half-zips pair lofted orchid yarn with breathable inner channels, trapping warmth before your warm-up while venting heat once your pace climbs.

Tops are cut with dropped shoulders and generous chest ease for effortless layering—take your true size for the signature studio silhouette, or size down one for a closer athletic fit.

Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle inside out with zippers closed, skip fabric softeners, and dry flat to preserve the lofty rib structure and deep orchid-to-lilac pigment.

In-stock sets dispatch within 24 hours on weekdays. Unworn pieces with tags still attached can be exchanged for a different size or returned free within 30 days.

Seasonal knit capsules are produced in small numbered batches. Core lilac sets restock monthly, while limited plum and orchid dye lots return only during winter seasonal drops.
